What AuditBoard (now Optro) is

AuditBoard now trades as Optro; its site shows 'formerly AuditBoard' and offers audit, risk, infosec and compliance solutions. Its SOX management page describes AI-run control testing on uploaded evidence, continuous control monitoring, an out-of-the-box SOX risk and control matrix, and workflows for both first-line teams and external auditors. It targets large enterprises and cites Fortune 500 customers.

Where AuditBoard (now Optro) is stronger

  • A full SOX programme: risk and control matrix, planning documentation, fieldwork and reporting in one product.
  • Testing support: the site describes AI agents running attribute tests on evidence such as user access reviews and reconciliations.
  • Auditor collaboration: workflows and permissions let external auditors complete tasks themselves.
  • Market position: Optro cites a Leader placement in the 2025 Gartner Magic Quadrant for GRC Tools and the Forrester Wave for GRC Platforms, Q2 2026.

Where docstoc fits

  • AR chase only: docstoc records the actor's email and role on every chase event and SOX action.
  • Dual control without a programme rollout: turn on SoD, invite a teammate, and the requester cannot approve their own send.
  • A pack your auditor can verify alone: frozen HTML, SHA-256 digest and an OpenTimestamps proof.
  • Plain price: Business is $39.99 a month at the time of writing.

Which one to choose

Choose AuditBoard (Optro) if you run a SOX programme with an RCM, testing cycles, remediation and external auditors working in the same tool. It is built for teams that treat SOX as a continuing process across the company.

Add docstoc if the open question is narrower: show that overdue-invoice follow-ups were requested, approved and sent by different people. docstoc is not a GRC suite, and it does not manage your RCM.

FAQ

Is AuditBoard the same as Optro?

Yes, according to Optro's own site, which describes itself as formerly AuditBoard. The login link still points to an auditboardapp.com address.

Can docstoc handle segregation of duties?

Yes, for chase sends. With SoD on, the requester cannot approve their own send and the API rejects self-approval. It does not cover segregation across your whole ERP.

Do I need AuditBoard if I use docstoc?

Not for AR chase evidence. For a wider SOX programme with testing and remediation, docstoc does not replace it and it does not claim to.
