A Paidnice alternative if you want to write and send the email yourself

Paidnice automates reminders on your ledger; docstoc drafts one email at a time and leaves sending to you.

What Paidnice is

Paidnice is accounts receivable automation for Xero and QuickBooks. It applies your payment terms to your invoices and chases them before, on and after the due date. It also emails statements, adds late fees your terms allow, reads customer replies, offers a payment portal and escalates overdue accounts to a person. Its own page says nothing runs until you approve the policies.

Where Paidnice is stronger

  • Reminders, statements and late fees run on a schedule from your ledger, so the work happens without you.
  • Customer replies are read and logged, with promises and questions noted and next steps drafted.
  • A payment portal with card, bank transfer and payment plans.
  • Usage-based tiers by invoice volume, with multi-currency, webhooks and an API on Pro.

Where docstoc fits

  • You want to see and edit each email before it leaves your inbox.
  • You send a handful of reminders a month, where a $69 plan is more than the job needs.
  • You are not on Xero or QuickBooks and prefer CSV or pasting an invoice.
  • You want free templates for payment reminders and contracts next to the drafts.

Which one to choose

Choose Paidnice if you invoice in Xero or QuickBooks, have enough invoices that manual chasing eats hours, and want late fees, statements and a payment portal handled for you. Volume-based pricing fits that use.

Choose docstoc if you want a good first draft and the final say. It does not chase on a schedule, apply late fees or post to your ledger.

FAQ

Does Paidnice send emails without my approval?

Not before you approve its policies. Its page says nothing runs until you approve them, and fees can be set to wait for your approval. After that, scheduled reminders run on their own.

Is docstoc a cheaper Paidnice?

On price, yes: $14.99 a month against $69 at the time of writing. The products differ, because docstoc does not automate sending, fees or statements.

Can I use both?

Yes, but not on the same invoice at the same time. Pause Paidnice reminders for any invoice you chase with a docstoc draft so the client gets one voice.
