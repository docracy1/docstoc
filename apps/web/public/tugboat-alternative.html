Tugboat Logic is now part of OneTrust, docstoc is a file certificate

If Tugboat Logic was your audit tool, check OneTrust first: docstoc covers only the document-proof part.

Document certificates See pricing

What Tugboat Logic is

Tugboat Logic was a security assurance platform that automated information security policies, audit readiness and security questionnaires for frameworks such as ISO 27001, SOC 2, CMMC, HIPAA and PCI DSS. OneTrust announced its acquisition on 21 September 2021 and said Tugboat Logic had about 800 customers. OneTrust described it as an entry point that smaller security teams could grow from into its enterprise GRC solutions.

Where Tugboat Logic is stronger

  • Policy creation, audit readiness and questionnaire answers, none of which docstoc does.
  • Framework support for ISO 27001, SOC 2, CMMC, HIPAA and PCI DSS, per OneTrust's announcement.
  • A route into OneTrust's wider GRC, privacy and third party risk products.
  • The backing of an established vendor with over 10,000 customers, as OneTrust stated in 2021.

Where docstoc fits

  • You want a record that a document existed in a specific form, shareable by link.
  • You are moving away from a security assurance tool and only need file integrity proof.
  • Certificates are free to create and check, and need no account to verify.
  • You also want chase drafts for overdue invoices that you send from your own mail client.

Which one to choose

Choose OneTrust's offering that grew out of Tugboat Logic if you want automated policy and audit readiness work, or a path towards a wider GRC and privacy platform.

Choose docstoc if what you actually need is smaller: to fix a document in time, share a verify link and keep invoice chase drafts in one place. It does not manage a security programme.

FAQ

Is Tugboat Logic still available?

Not as a separate product, according to what we could confirm. OneTrust announced the acquisition in 2021 and positioned Tugboat Logic as a step into its GRC platform. Check with OneTrust for the current name and plans.

Does docstoc replace Tugboat Logic?

No. docstoc does not replace Tugboat Logic. It certifies individual files with a SHA-256 fingerprint and a public verification page, and it does not map controls or run compliance checks.

Can docstoc import my Tugboat Logic data?

No. There is no importer. Export the files you want to keep provable and certify them in docstoc.
