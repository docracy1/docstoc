What Bonsai is

Bonsai is a client and project management tool. Its pricing page lists Basic, Essentials, Premium and Elite plans, priced per user per month, with time tracking on all plans and proposals, contracts and invoices from Essentials up. Its site also shows a template library of 500+ contracts, proposals, invoices and briefs. It is built for people who want one tool for the whole client workflow.

Where Bonsai is stronger

  • Client workflow: proposals, contracts, invoices and payments in one tool from Essentials up.
  • Time tracking on every plan, with timesheet locking on Elite.
  • Templates filtered by industry, covering contracts, proposals, scopes of work and timesheets.
  • A 7 day full-access trial and onboarding support for teams of 10+ users.

Where docstoc fits

  • You need a contract or policy but not a project suite: 1,000+ free templates across business, legal, real estate, finance and HR.
  • You work alone or in a pair and prefer a flat workspace price over per-user pricing.
  • You want a hash certificate for the signed PDF, with a public verify page.
  • You want AI-drafted reminder emails for late invoices, which you review and send yourself.

Which one to choose

Choose Bonsai if you want proposals, contracts, invoices and time tracking in one place and bill clients from the same tool. The per-user price buys that workflow.

Choose docstoc if you only need the document or the follow-up. Copy a contract, certify the final PDF, and keep your project tracking wherever it is today.

FAQ

Is Bonsai cheaper than docstoc?

It depends on the plan and seats. At the time of writing Basic is $9 per user per month billed annually, but contracts and invoices are listed from Essentials at $19. docstoc Pro is $14.99 per month for the workspace.

Does Bonsai have free contract templates?

We could not confirm it. Bonsai shows a library of 500+ templates, but the page does not say whether they are free, and contract templates link to a sign up page.

Can I move Bonsai contracts to docstoc?

Download them as PDF from Bonsai and keep them. docstoc can certify those files and offers templates for the next contract.
