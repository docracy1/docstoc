What Workiva is

Workiva is a cloud platform for connected reporting and compliance work. Its SOX material describes control documentation, testing and evidence kept in one system, alongside SEC reporting. A Crane Co. customer story says the company centralised audit, SOX and SEC data in it and replaced email approvals. It is built for public companies with internal audit and reporting teams, and for the advisers who support them.

Where Workiva is stronger

  • Breadth: audit, SOX and SEC data in one system, as the Crane Co. customer story describes it.
  • Internal controls across frameworks: control governance, automated testing, and evidence analysis and remediation, on one platform with audit, risk and finance.
  • Live workpapers: the same story reports managers seeing workpaper status without chasing it by email.
  • Scale: Crane Co. reports nearly 200 documents in the platform in 2019, far beyond what a single AR workflow needs.

Where docstoc fits

  • One narrow control area: who requested, approved and sent overdue-invoice follow-ups, with the actor's email and role on every event.
  • Maker-checker on chase sends: the requester cannot approve their own send, and an approval cannot be reused afterwards.
  • A frozen auditor pack for a date range (HTML, SHA-256 and an OpenTimestamps .ots proof) that an auditor can check without trusting docstoc's database.
  • A flat plan: Business is $39.99 a month at the time of writing, with no sales call.

Which one to choose

Choose Workiva if your SOX programme, SEC reporting and internal audit work need to sit in one connected system, and you have a team to run it. It fits companies where the question is the whole control environment, not one process.

Add docstoc if your gap is narrow: auditors sample AR follow-ups and you cannot show who approved them. It does not replace workpapers or reporting. It gives you one verifiable file for the chase process.

FAQ

Can docstoc replace Workiva for SOX?

No. docstoc covers only accounts receivable chase evidence. Workiva covers broader control documentation, testing and reporting, so most teams would use the two side by side.

Does docstoc make us SOX compliant?

No. It produces evidence about chase activity and maker-checker approvals. Whether your controls meet SOX is a decision for you, your auditor and your advisers.

How does my auditor check the pack?

By hashing the HTML file and matching it to the SHA-256 digest, then verifying the .ots proof against Bitcoin. That check does not need a docstoc login.
