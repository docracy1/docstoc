FAQs

Is this a legal-entity or business-registry check?

No. The badge confirms DNS control of a domain (via a real Let's Encrypt certificate issued through docstoc) and, once Bitcoin-confirmed, the date that verified status began. It does not check company registries or claim KYC/identity verification.

Do I need an account to look up someone else's badge?

No. Paste a workspace account ID or a /trust/… link below — the lookup is public. Getting your own badge requires securing a domain in docstoc first.

Where does the embed script go?

Anywhere HTML is allowed — your website footer, proposals, or a client portal. The script loads a small domain-verified badge that links to the public trust profile.

When does the Bitcoin timestamp show up?

Usually within a few hours of first verification. Until then the badge still says domain-verified; once confirmed it upgrades to include the verified-since date.
