What Hyperproof is

Hyperproof is a compliance and risk platform. Its site lists risk management, compliance, audit, third-party risk and policy management, plus a FedRAMP-hosted Hyperproof Gov. It cites 160+ pre-built frameworks, including SOC 2, ISO 27001, NIST and HIPAA, and 200+ integrations. It names mid-market and enterprise GRC teams in government, healthcare, technology and fintech as its audience.

Where Hyperproof is stronger

  • Framework breadth: 160+ pre-built frameworks and custom ones, so one control set can serve several audits.
  • Integrations: the site cites 200+ and its Hypersyncs for collecting evidence.
  • Audit workflow: evidence can be linked to auditor requests, with secure collaboration with auditors.
  • Government use: Hyperproof Gov runs in a FedRAMP Certified Class C (Rev5) environment, per the site.

Where docstoc fits

  • Finance, not IT compliance: docstoc covers who chased which invoice and who approved the send.
  • Maker-checker on chase sends, with the requester blocked from approving their own request.
  • Retention window, legal hold and optional purge for the chase evidence.
  • A pack frozen per period, with SHA-256 and an OpenTimestamps proof the auditor can verify.

Which one to choose

Choose Hyperproof if you manage several frameworks at once, such as SOC 2 and ISO 27001, and want evidence collected through integrations and shared with auditors. It suits security and compliance teams with a programme to run.

Add docstoc if you are in finance and need a narrow thing: proof that invoice follow-ups were approved by a second person and not rewritten later. It is not a GRC tool and does not map your controls to frameworks.

FAQ

Does Hyperproof support SOX?

We could not confirm it. The pages we read do not mention SOX, though they list 160+ frameworks. Ask Hyperproof which SOX workflows it offers before you decide.

Can docstoc replace Hyperproof?

No. docstoc has no framework library and no integrations for IT evidence. It covers only the evidence trail for AR invoice chasing.

Is the auditor pack proof of compliance?

No. It shows who did what and that the file was not changed after creation. Whether your controls are adequate is for you and your auditor to judge.
