FAQ

Does docstoc send chase emails for me?

No. docstoc drafts payment reminders and follow-ups — you review and send from your own inbox. Clients always hear from you.

Where should I start if I have one overdue invoice?

Paste the invoice in the app for a tone-matched draft, or copy a free template from the library. Match tone to days overdue: friendly first, firmer later.

Is invoice chasing only part of docstoc?

Yes — docstoc is the Trust Automation Layer: templates, invoicing, SSL, document certificates, and AI collections in one platform. This hub focuses on getting paid.
