A Chaser alternative for people who write their own chasers

Chaser runs receivables for finance teams; docstoc only drafts the email, so it costs far less and you keep the send button.

AI chasing See pricing

What Chaser is

Chaser is accounts receivable automation and cash flow forecasting software. It chases invoices by email, SMS and automated calls, offers a customer payment portal, credit reports and late payment fees, and syncs with Xero, QuickBooks Online, Sage, NetSuite and others. It also offers debt collection services and a managed credit control service. Plans are tiered by company revenue, which suits finance teams with hundreds of open invoices.

Where Chaser is stronger

  • Chasing beyond email: SMS, automated phone calls and letters, with call recording on higher plans.
  • Two-way sync with accounting systems such as Sage, NetSuite, SAP and Dynamics 365 Business Central, not only Xero and QuickBooks.
  • A payment portal and Chaser Pay, so customers can pay from the reminder.
  • Credit monitoring, cash flow forecasting and optional human credit control through Chaser Care.

Where docstoc fits

  • You chase a few dozen invoices and mainly need the wording right for the number of days overdue.
  • You want to read every email before it goes out. docstoc never sends for you.
  • You would rather pay a flat $14.99 a month than choose a plan by company revenue.
  • You also need document templates or a certificate for a file, which come with the same account.

Which one to choose

Choose Chaser if several people handle receivables, you want automated calls and SMS, need a payment portal, or want credit reports and a team to take over collections. Its sync with larger accounting systems matters at that size.

Choose docstoc if you send your own reminders and want help with tone and timing. It is a drafting tool, not an automated sender, and it does not replace credit control or debt collection.

FAQ

Is docstoc cheaper than Chaser?

Yes, on published prices at the time of writing. Chaser's Compact plan is £199 a month; docstoc Pro is $14.99 a month. They do different jobs: Chaser automates the whole chase, docstoc drafts the email.

Can docstoc send reminders automatically like Chaser?

No. docstoc writes the email and you send it from your own mailbox. That is deliberate, so no client hears from you without your say.

Can I move my open invoices from Chaser?

Yes. Export unpaid invoices as CSV or connect QuickBooks or Xero on Pro, then generate drafts. Turn off Chaser's automated sending first so clients do not get two reminders.
