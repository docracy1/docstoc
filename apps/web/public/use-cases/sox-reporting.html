SOX reporting for accounts receivable

Give controllers and auditors attributable chase evidence — who approved what, when it happened, and a Bitcoin-anchored proof the file was not rewritten later.

What Ready / Partial / Missing mean

  • Ready — enough evidence or settings are in place for that control in your workspace.
  • Partial — the capability exists, but you still need chase activity, a teammate, or more history.
  • Missing — not enabled yet, or no evidence has been produced (e.g. SoD off, or no daily anchors yet).

These labels are live readiness for your account — not a product roadmap checklist.

Where is the final auditor file?

In the app: SOX reporting → Evidence & exports → choose dates → Create timestamped pack. Download and send .html + .sha256 + .ots together.

What ships in the dashboard

  • Control coverage overview — readiness across trails, anchors, SoD, retention, and certificates.
  • Attributable audit trail — actor email/role on chase events and SOX actions.
  • Maker-checker (SoD) — optional dual control before marking a chase sent.
  • Daily hash anchors — chase + open/click tracking hashed and OpenTimestamps-anchored.
  • Timestamped auditor packs — frozen HTML + SHA-256 + .ots for a date range.

Who it is for

Finance teams that chase overdue invoices and need ICFR-friendly evidence for external auditors, internal audit, or month-end close — without a full GRC suite.

FAQ

Is this a full SOX compliance platform?

No. docstoc focuses on tamper-evident AR chase evidence and dual-control send workflows — the part of SOX ICFR that touches invoice collections.

Which plans include SOX reporting?

Business ($39.99/mo). Free and Pro users see an upgrade prompt in the dashboard.

How long until Bitcoin confirmation?

OpenTimestamps calendars usually confirm within hours. Pending packs remain downloadable; the .ots upgrades when calendars publish.
