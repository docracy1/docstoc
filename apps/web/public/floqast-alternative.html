What FloQast is

FloQast is accounting software built around the month-end close. Its site lists close management, account reconciliations, AI-assisted matching and journal entries, and a Risk and Compliance package with financial controls covering SOX scoping, controls and testing. It names controllers, CFOs, internal audit and accountants as its audience, and lists ERP connections including NetSuite, SAP, Oracle and QuickBooks.

Where FloQast is stronger

  • The close itself: checklists, reconciliations and journal entry management are core products, not add-ons.
  • ERP reach: the site lists Workday, NetSuite, SAP, Oracle, Yardi, Sage Intacct, Xero and QuickBooks.
  • Financial controls module: FloQast describes SOX scoping, controls and testing, plus audit readiness.
  • Pricing structure: the pricing page says no per-user fees, with three packages sold through sales.

Where docstoc fits

  • Chase activity sits outside the close. docstoc records who drafted, requested, approved and marked each overdue-invoice follow-up as sent.
  • Maker-checker on sends: the requester cannot approve their own chase, and each approval is used once.
  • Period tests against a seeded AR control library, with pass, fail or exception results.
  • Auditor packs frozen per date range with a SHA-256 digest and an OpenTimestamps proof.

Which one to choose

Choose FloQast if the close is where your team loses time: reconciliations, checklists, journals and controls tied to the ledger. It suits finance teams already working in a supported ERP and willing to talk to sales for pricing.

Add docstoc if collections is the missing piece: follow-ups happen in inboxes and auditors ask who approved them. It sits next to FloQast and does not touch your ledger or reconciliations.

FAQ

Does docstoc replace FloQast?

No. FloQast covers the close and financial controls. docstoc covers only the evidence trail for invoice chasing, so the two do different jobs.

Does docstoc connect to my ERP?

Not to the ERPs FloQast lists. docstoc offers QuickBooks Online and Xero connectors on Pro and Business, plus CSV and PDF import, per its plan list.

What does FloQast cost?

FloQast does not publish prices. Its pricing page lists three packages and says there are no per-user fees. docstoc Business is $39.99 a month at the time of writing.
