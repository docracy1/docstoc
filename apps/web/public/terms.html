docstoc is a tool that drafts payment follow-up emails from invoice details you provide. The free tier includes a limited number of AI drafts per month; a paid plan removes that limit.

docstoc does not email your clients. Drafts are for you to copy, edit, and send from your own inbox. You're responsible for what you send.
