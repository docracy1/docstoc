How it works

  1. Find the document. Search by name or browse by occasion.
  2. Fill it in. Edit the text right on the page. Every template says which US rules it was written for.
  3. Save it as PDF. Download, print or send it. Your edits stay in your browser.

Templates are a starting point, not legal advice. For anything with real money or risk behind it, have a lawyer in your state read the final version.

A client is paying late?

Paste the invoice details and docstoc drafts the reminder for how late it is: a friendly note in the first week, a firmer one after that, and a direct reminder once it is more than 30 days late. You copy it into your own email. docstoc never writes to your clients.

FAQ

What is docstoc?

A library of business and legal document templates for US small businesses: contracts, letters, notices and forms. You can read, edit and download every template for free.

Do I need an account?

No. Reading, editing and saving a template as PDF works without signing up. You need an account for AI drafts and for Pro.

Are the templates legal advice?

No. Each template is a starting point and shows which US rules it was written for. Laws differ by state, so have a lawyer check documents with real money or risk behind them.

Does docstoc send emails to my clients?

No. Payment reminders are drafts you copy into your own email. Your clients hear from you, not from us.

Is this the original docstoc?

No. The original docstoc closed in 2015. This docstoc is run by RELACON GmbH and is not affiliated with it. See About and what happened to the original docstoc.
