What it can do

docstoc automates real Let's Encrypt domain-validated certificates for hostnames you control — one DNS TXT record, renewals in-product, same account as invoices and documents.

  • Add a domain — Free: 5 × 90-day LE certs; Pro: multi-SAN; Business: wildcards + volume.
  • DNS-01 validation — publish TXT record(s); no HTTP file drop or nameserver move.
  • Auto-renew — reissue before expiry while your plan stays active.
  • Download PEMs — install on nginx, Apache, Caddy, or your host panel.

How it works

1. Add your domain

Sign in and add the hostname you want secured in SSL certificates.

2. Publish one DNS TXT

Copy the challenge to your DNS provider. No CDN proxy required.

3. Install & renew

Download PEMs for your server; renewals stay in the product.

Who it’s for

  • Freelancers and small businesses who need HTTPS without running certbot
  • Agencies putting client sites or portals on custom domains
  • Teams that already use docstoc for invoices or documents and want SSL in the same account
Honest limits
  • Automation layer, not a reseller — the CA is Let's Encrypt; you pay for managed issuance/renewal in your workspace.
  • DV only — Let's Encrypt domain validation. Not OV/EV.
  • Not a commercial CA replacement for warranties or enterprise PKI.
  • Not your host/CDN — keep hosting; docstoc automates the certificate lifecycle.

FAQ

Is this a free SSL certificate?

The CA (Let's Encrypt) does not charge for the DV cert. docstoc is an automation layer: Free includes 5 × 90-day certs; Pro/Business unlock multi-SAN, wildcards, and higher limits.

Are you a certificate reseller?

No. We do not resell commercial CA SKUs. We manage Let's Encrypt ACME orders for domains you control; you pay for the automation and workspace.

SSL or TLS — which do I need?

Modern browsers use TLS. People still search “SSL certificate” for the same HTTPS padlock. Same product either way.

Do I need to move my site to docstoc hosting?

No. Keep your host. Add DNS TXT record(s); renewals are handled in-product.

Can I get OV or EV certificates?

Not from docstoc. We automate DV Let's Encrypt certificates. Use a commercial CA if procurement requires OV/EV.
