FreeSSL.org or docstoc: ACME tooling or a managed Let's Encrypt workflow

Both give you free-to-start HTTPS certificates: FreeSSL.org leans on ACME and a REST API, docstoc on one DNS record and a workspace.

SSL product See pricing

What FreeSSL.org is

FreeSSL.org describes itself as a free SSL certificate provider with ACME tools. At the time of writing its site lists 90-day and 1-year single-domain, multi-domain and wildcard certificates, and four plans: Free at $0 a month, Basic at $9.9, Pro at $99.9 and Enterprise on request. It says issuance takes 1 to 5 minutes, renewal is automated, and ACME integrations plus a REST API are available. It suits people who want to script certificates themselves.

Where FreeSSL.org is stronger

  • A REST API is listed on every plan, which helps if certificate requests are part of your own tooling.
  • It lists 1-year certificates as well as 90-day ones, which docstoc does not offer.
  • It lists a higher tier for volume: Pro at $99.9 a month and an Enterprise plan with custom solutions.
  • Technical support is listed on all plans, including Free.

Where docstoc fits

  • Free includes 5 Let's Encrypt certificates of 90 days each. Pro adds multi-SAN certificates and Business adds wildcards and higher volume.
  • You publish one DNS TXT record (DNS-01), download PEM files and install them on nginx, Apache, Caddy or your host panel.
  • Renewals are handled in the product while your plan stays active, in the same account as invoice follow-ups and documents.
  • Pricing is a flat Pro plan at $14.99 a month or Business at $39.99 a month, shown on the docstoc pricing page at the time of writing.

Which one to choose

Choose FreeSSL.org if you want an API on every plan, 1-year certificates, or a dedicated certificate service with higher tiers for many domains. It fits developers who build certificate requests into their own scripts.

Choose docstoc if you already use it for invoices or documents and want HTTPS for a domain in the same account, with one TXT record and renewals handled. It stays DV only on Let's Encrypt.

FAQ

Is FreeSSL.org free?

It has a Free plan at $0 a month, and paid Basic and Pro plans at the time of writing. Check its pricing page for the current limits.

Which certificate authority does docstoc use?

Let's Encrypt. docstoc is an automation layer, not a reseller or a CA, so you get DV certificates only.

Can I move a domain from FreeSSL.org to docstoc?

Yes. Add the hostname in docstoc, publish the TXT record, install the new PEM files, check HTTPS, then stop the old certificate. Never email a private key.
