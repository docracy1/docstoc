Notice of Meeting

Organization: [Organization Name] · Meeting Type: [Board / Shareholder / Membership / Annual] · Date Issued: [Date]

Notice is hereby given that a [meeting type] meeting of [Organization Name] will be held as follows:

1. Meeting Details

Date: [Date]
Time: [Time]
Location: [Physical Address or Virtual Meeting Link/Dial-In]

2. Purpose

[State the general purpose — e.g., to elect directors, approve the annual budget, vote on a proposed bylaw amendment, or conduct regular business.]

3. Agenda

  1. Call to order and roll call
  2. Approval of minutes from [Previous Meeting Date]
  3. [Agenda Item]
  4. [Agenda Item]
  5. New business
  6. Adjournment

4. Quorum

A quorum of [Number/Percentage] of [directors/members/shareholders] is required to conduct business. If a quorum is not present, the meeting may be adjourned to [Alternate Date].

5. Proxy Voting

[State whether proxy voting is permitted and how a proxy form may be submitted, if applicable.]

6. RSVP

Please confirm attendance by [RSVP Date] to [Contact Name] at [Email/Phone].

Issued by: ______________________ Title: ____________ Date: ____________

This document is provided for informational and educational purposes only and does not constitute legal advice. Required notice periods, quorum rules, and permissible delivery methods (mail, email, posting) are usually set by your bylaws or state nonprofit/corporate code — confirm before sending.
