General Warranty Deed

Grantor: [Seller Name] · Grantee: [Buyer Name] · Date: [Date]

1. Conveyance

For and in consideration of [Amount] and other good and valuable consideration, receipt of which is acknowledged, Grantor hereby grants, bargains, sells, and conveys to Grantee, and Grantee's heirs and assigns forever, the following described real property located in [County], [State]:

[Legal Description of Property — attach as Exhibit A if lengthy]

Commonly known as: [Street Address]

2. Warranty of Title

Grantor warrants that Grantor is lawfully seized of the property in fee simple, has good right to convey it, and that the property is free from all encumbrances except: [List any known easements, restrictions, or liens].

3. Full Warranty

Grantor covenants to warrant and defend title to the property against the lawful claims of all persons whomsoever, for all periods of ownership including those before Grantor acquired the property.

4. Habendum

To have and to hold the property, together with all rights and appurtenances, to Grantee and Grantee's heirs and assigns forever.

Grantor Signature: ______________________ Date: ____________
[Notary Acknowledgment Block — required for recording]

This document is provided for informational and educational purposes only and does not constitute legal advice. A general warranty deed must be signed, notarized, and recorded with the county recorder's office to be effective — requirements vary by state, so have a real estate attorney or title company prepare or review the final version.
