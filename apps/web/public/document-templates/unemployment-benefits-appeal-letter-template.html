[State Unemployment Office/Appeals Board]
[Address]

RE: Appeal of Determination — Claim #[Number], [Your Name]

To Whom It May Concern,

I am writing to formally appeal the determination dated [Date], which [denied my claim / determined I was ineligible for benefits], on the following basis: [State the stated reason for denial].

Grounds for Appeal

[Explain specifically why you believe the determination was incorrect — e.g. "I was laid off due to a company-wide reduction in force, not terminated for misconduct as stated."]

Supporting Evidence

[List documents attached — termination letter, separation notice, witness statements, etc.]

I request that this determination be reversed and that my claim for benefits be approved. Please advise me of the date and process for any hearing related to this appeal.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

Before you write the appeal

  • Find the deadline on your determination letter. Appeal windows are often short, sometimes only a couple of weeks, and a late appeal can be rejected without review.
  • Read the stated reason for the denial carefully. Your letter should answer that reason, not your whole employment history.
  • Collect evidence: termination or separation letter, emails, schedules, pay slips, names of witnesses.
  • Keep claiming or certifying for benefits while the appeal runs, if your agency requires it.

How to make the letter work

Keep it short and factual. Name the determination date and claim number, state that you appeal, and explain in a few sentences why the decision is wrong, for example that you were laid off in a reduction in force rather than dismissed for misconduct. List the documents you attach and ask for a hearing.

Send it the way the agency asks, often online or by mail, and keep proof of the date you sent it.

Outside the US

The steps differ by country. In the UK, for most DWP benefit decisions you first ask for a mandatory reconsideration before you can appeal to a tribunal. Check the official guidance for your benefit and country.

FAQ

How long do I have to appeal an unemployment decision?

It depends on your state or country. The deadline is printed on the determination; treat it as strict.

What should I say in an unemployment appeal letter?

That you disagree with the determination of a specific date, the specific reason it is wrong, and which evidence supports you. Facts work better than emotions.

Do I need a lawyer to appeal?

Most people appeal on their own. Free legal aid or worker advice organisations can help if your case involves misconduct allegations.
