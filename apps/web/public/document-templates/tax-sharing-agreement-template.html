Tax Sharing Agreement

This Agreement is made on [Date] between [Parent Company Name] ("Parent") and [Subsidiary/Affiliate Name] ("Affiliate"), members of an affiliated group filing a consolidated tax return.

1. Purpose

This Agreement establishes how tax liabilities, benefits, and payments are allocated among the Parties when filing a [consolidated/combined] return, so each Party bears a share reflecting its own operations.

2. Allocation Method

Each Party's share of the group's consolidated tax liability is calculated as if it had filed a separate return, using [Allocation Method, e.g. the "separate return method"], applied consistently each tax period.

3. Payment of Allocated Tax

Each Affiliate pays Parent its allocated share within [Number] days of the filing or estimated payment due date. Parent remains responsible for filing and remitting the consolidated return to the tax authority.

4. Tax Benefits

Where an Affiliate's losses or credits reduce the group's overall liability, Parent compensates that Affiliate for the value of the benefit used, calculated as [Method].

5. Audits and Adjustments

If a tax authority adjusts the group's liability following an audit, the Parties will reallocate the adjustment consistent with the method in Section 2.

6. Term and Termination

This Agreement remains in effect while the Parties file as part of the same consolidated group, and terminates if an Affiliate leaves the group, subject to a final true-up of amounts owed.

7. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of [State/Country].

Parent: ______________________ Date: ____________ Affiliate: ______________________ Date: ____________

*This document is provided for informational and educational purposes only and does not constitute tax or legal advice. Tax sharing arrangements have significant tax and accounting consequences — consult a qualified tax advisor or attorney before adopting one.*
