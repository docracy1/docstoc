Problem Statement: [Project/Initiative Name]

Prepared by: [Name] · Date: [Date]

1. Current Situation

[Describe what is happening today, in neutral, factual terms — no solution talk yet.]

2. The Problem

[State the specific problem in one or two sentences. Be precise: who is affected, what is going wrong, and since when.]

3. Evidence

[Data, examples, or observations that demonstrate the problem is real and significant — not assumed.]

4. Impact if Unaddressed

[What happens if this problem continues — cost, risk, missed opportunity, affected stakeholders.]

5. Scope

In scope: [What this problem statement covers]
Out of scope: [What it deliberately does not cover, to keep the problem bounded]

6. Desired Outcome

[What "solved" looks like — described as a result, not as a specific solution.]

Separating Section 6 (desired outcome) from any mention of a specific solution is the single most common fix this document needs — jumping to "we need to build X" here skips the step of confirming everyone agrees on what problem X is even supposed to solve.

FAQ

Is this problem statement template really free?

Yes — this Problem Statement template on docstoc is free to view, edit online, and download as a PDF with no account or signup required.

What does this problem statement cover?

This business template includes 1. Current Situation, 2. The Problem, 3. Evidence, 4. Impact if Unaddressed. Fill in the [bracketed] placeholders, edit any clause online, then download a PDF — the docstoc.io footer stays on every export.

Is this legal advice?

No. This is a general-purpose starting structure, not advice for your specific situation. Business agreements can hinge on details — state of formation, industry-specific regulation, how a dispute would actually play out — that a template can't account for. Have a business attorney review anything that involves real money or real risk before you rely on it.

Can I edit the wording?

Yes — edit directly on the page, adjust any section or clause, and re-fill the 4 placeholder fields to match your situation before downloading a PDF. It's a starting structure, not a rigid script.
