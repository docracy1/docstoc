Revocable Living Trust

Grantor/Settlor: [Name]
Trustee: [Name] (initially the Grantor; Successor Trustee: [Name])
Trust Name: The [Grantor Name] Revocable Living Trust
Date: [Date]

1. Trust Property

The Grantor transfers the property listed on Schedule A (the "Trust Property") to the Trustee, to be held, managed, and distributed under this Agreement.

2. Revocability

During the Grantor's lifetime, this trust is revocable. The Grantor may amend or revoke it at any time by written instrument delivered to the Trustee.

3. Lifetime Distributions

During the Grantor's life, the Trustee shall pay income and principal to or for the benefit of the Grantor as the Grantor directs.

4. Incapacity

If the Grantor becomes incapacitated, the Successor Trustee shall manage the trust for the Grantor's benefit according to this Agreement.

5. Distribution on Death

On the Grantor's death, the Successor Trustee shall distribute the Trust Property to: [Beneficiary names and shares], after paying debts, taxes, and expenses.

6. Successor Trustee

If the Trustee is unable or unwilling to serve, [Successor Trustee Name] shall serve without bond.

Signature: ______________________ Date: ____________
[Grantor Name]

Signature: ______________________ Date: ____________
[Trustee Name]

A living trust avoids probate only for assets actually retitled into the trust's name — have an attorney confirm your state's funding and execution requirements.
