Request for Dismissal

Court: [Court Name] · Case No.: [Case Number]
Plaintiff: [Plaintiff Name] v. Defendant: [Defendant Name]

1. Party Requesting Dismissal

[Name of party], [Plaintiff/Defendant] in the above-captioned action, respectfully requests dismissal as set forth below.

2. Scope of Dismissal

This request covers (select one):

  • [ ] The entire action, as to all parties
  • [ ] Only the following claim(s) or party(ies): [Specify]

3. Type of Dismissal

  • [ ] With prejudice (the claim(s) may not be refiled)
  • [ ] Without prejudice (the claim(s) may be refiled)

4. Basis for Request

[Reason for the dismissal, e.g. settlement reached, claim resolved, party withdrawing voluntarily.]

5. Settlement Reference (if applicable)

[If dismissal follows a settlement, reference the settlement agreement date and key terms being fulfilled.]

6. Costs

[State whether each party bears its own costs and fees, or reference another arrangement.]

7. Certification

I certify that a copy of this Request has been served on all parties or their counsel of record on [Date].

Signature: ______________________ Date: ____________
[Name], [Plaintiff/Defendant/Attorney for ______]

This document is a general template, not a substitute for your court's official dismissal form. Court rules and required forms vary by jurisdiction — confirm local requirements or consult an attorney before filing.
