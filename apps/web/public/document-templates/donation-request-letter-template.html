Donation Request Letter

[Your Name/Organization] [Address] [Date]

[Recipient Name] [Recipient Company/Organization] [Address]

Dear [Recipient Name],

1. Introduction

[Briefly introduce your organization/cause and the event or program for which you are seeking support — e.g., a charity auction, community fundraiser, or sponsorship drive.]

2. The Ask

We are requesting a donation of [cash gift / specific item or service] to support [Event/Program Name], taking place on [Date] at [Location]. Your contribution will help us [describe impact — e.g., fund scholarships, cover event costs, stock the auction].

3. What You'll Receive

In recognition of your generosity, [Organization Name] will [list benefits — e.g., recognition on event materials, a thank-you mention on social media, a tax-deductible receipt].

4. Tax Information

[Organization Name] is a [501(c)(3) / registered nonprofit], Tax ID [EIN Number]. Donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

5. How to Respond

Please let us know by [Response Deadline] whether you're able to help. You can reach us at [Phone/Email], or mail your donation to the address above.

Thank you for considering this request — your support makes a real difference.

Sincerely, [Your Name], [Title]

*This document is provided for informational and educational purposes only and does not constitute tax or legal advice. Confirm your organization's tax-exempt status and donor-acknowledgment requirements with a qualified accountant before issuing receipts.*
