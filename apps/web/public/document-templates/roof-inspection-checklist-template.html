Property Address: [Address] · Inspector: [Name] · Date: [Date] · Roof Age (if known): [Years]

Exterior/Overall Condition

[ ] Missing, cracked, or curling shingles/tiles noted [ ] Granule loss on shingles (indicates aging) assessed [ ] Flashing around chimneys, vents, and skylights intact [ ] Gutters and downspouts clear and properly attached [ ] Signs of sagging or structural deflection

Penetrations and Vents

[ ] Vent boots/seals intact, no cracking [ ] Chimney cap and mortar in good condition [ ] Skylight seals checked for leaks

Attic Inspection (if accessible)

[ ] No visible water staining or active leaks on the underside of the roof deck [ ] Adequate ventilation (soffit and ridge vents functioning) [ ] Insulation in acceptable condition, no signs of moisture damage [ ] No signs of pest intrusion through roof penetrations

Drainage

[ ] Roof slope directs water properly toward gutters/drains [ ] No standing water/ponding on flat or low-slope sections [ ] Downspouts direct water away from the foundation

Estimated Remaining Life

[Estimate based on material type, age, and condition observed]

Recommended Actions

[ ] No action needed at this time [ ] Minor repairs recommended: [List] [ ] Full roof replacement recommended: [Reasoning]

Inspector signature: ______________________ Date: ____________

*Checking the attic from the inside (not just looking at the roof from the ground or a ladder) is what actually catches an active leak early — water staining on the underside of the roof deck often shows up long before any damage is visible from outside, and it's the difference between a small repair and a full replacement.*
