Student Handbook — [School/Program Name]

Academic Year: [Year] · Effective Date: [Date]

1. Welcome

Welcome to [School/Program Name]. This handbook explains what's expected of students and what students can expect from us during [Year/Term].

2. Attendance

Students are expected to attend [Percentage]% of scheduled sessions. Absences must be reported to [Contact/Office] within [Timeframe].

3. Code of Conduct

Students are expected to treat others respectfully, avoid academic dishonesty, and follow campus/classroom rules. Violations may result in [a warning, suspension, or dismissal], per the disciplinary policy.

4. Grading and Assessment

Grading scale: [e.g., A–F or Pass/Fail]. Grades are posted [Frequency] via [Platform/System].

5. Health and Safety

[Emergency procedures, first-aid contact, and how to report an incident.]

6. Communication

Official communication is sent via [Email/Portal]. Students should check it [Frequency].

7. Grievance Procedure

A student with a concern should first raise it with [Instructor/Advisor], then escalate to [Office/Role] if unresolved.

8. Acknowledgment

I have received and read the [School/Program Name] Student Handbook.

Student Signature: ______________________ Date: ____________

Policies touching discipline, grades, or safety often need to match your state's education code or accreditor requirements — have this reviewed by your institution's compliance contact before distributing it as official policy.
