Advertising Agreement

Advertiser: [Company Name] Publisher/Platform: [Company Name] Effective Date: [Date] Term: [Start Date] – [End Date]

1. Advertising Placement

Publisher will display Advertiser's advertisements as follows: [Placement/platform, format, size, location].

2. Deliverables

ItemSpecificationFlight Dates
[Ad unit][Format/size][Dates]

3. Fees and Payment

Total fee: [Amount], payable [schedule — e.g. 50% on signing, 50% on campaign start]. Rates based on: [CPM/flat fee/other].

4. Creative Approval

Advertiser will supply creative materials by [Date]. Publisher may reject creative that violates its content guidelines, with written notice.

5. Performance Reporting

Publisher will provide performance reports (impressions, clicks, or other agreed metrics) [frequency, e.g. monthly].

6. Cancellation

Either party may cancel with [Number] days' written notice; fees for ads already run are non-refundable.

7. Signatures

Signature: ______________________ Date: ____________ [Advertiser Representative]

Signature: ______________________ Date: ____________ [Publisher Representative]

*Spell out what counts as a "run" ad if the campaign is cancelled mid-flight — this is the most common source of billing disputes in short-term ad deals.*

FAQ

Is this advertising agreement template really free?

Yes. Like every template in docstoc's library, this advertising agreement is free to use, with no account or signup needed to view, edit, or download it.

What does this advertising agreement cover?

This business template includes 1. Advertising Placement, 2. Deliverables, 3. Fees and Payment, 4. Creative Approval. Fill in the [bracketed] placeholders, edit any clause online, then download a PDF — the docstoc.io footer stays on every export.

Is this legal advice?

No. This is a general-purpose starting structure, not advice for your specific situation. Business agreements can hinge on details — state of formation, industry-specific regulation, how a dispute would actually play out — that a template can't account for. Have a business attorney review anything that involves real money or real risk before you rely on it.

Can I edit the wording?

Yes — edit directly on the page, adjust any section or clause, and re-fill the 15 placeholder fields to match your situation before downloading a PDF. It's a starting structure, not a rigid script.
