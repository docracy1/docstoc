Independent Living Agreement

This Agreement is made on [Date] between [Community Name] ("Community") and [Resident Name] ("Resident") for Unit [Unit Number/Description].

1. Nature of Services

This is an independent living arrangement. The Community does not provide skilled nursing, personal care, or medical services; Resident is responsible for arranging any such care independently unless a separate care agreement is signed.

2. Services Included

[e.g. "Meals in the dining room, weekly housekeeping, scheduled transportation, building maintenance, and access to common areas and activities."]

3. Monthly Fee

Monthly Fee: [Amount], due on the [Day] of each month. Fee may increase with [Notice period, e.g. "60 days'"] written notice.

4. Resident Responsibilities

Resident agrees to follow community rules, maintain the unit in good condition, and notify the Community of any change in care needs that may affect eligibility for independent living.

5. Move-Out & Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement with [Notice period] written notice. The Community may require Resident to transition to a higher level of care if independent living is no longer appropriate, subject to applicable state regulations.

6. Liability & Insurance

Resident is encouraged to maintain personal property and liability insurance. The Community is not responsible for loss of personal property except where caused by its negligence.

7. Emergency Contact

Name: [Name] · Relationship: [Relationship] · Phone: [Phone]

Community representative signature: ______________________ Date: ____________ Resident signature: ______________________ Date: ____________

*Independent living communities are regulated differently from assisted living or skilled nursing facilities, and requirements vary significantly by state — confirm licensing and disclosure requirements with local counsel before use.*
