Absolutely. Copy the subject and body, then adjust the tone, swap in your invoice details, or rewrite any part of it — it's a starting point, not a rigid script. The placeholders like [Client name] and [Invoice #] make it quick to personalize before sending.

When should I actually send this — is "90 days overdue" the right moment?
