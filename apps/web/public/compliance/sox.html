Open SOX reporting

What SOX covers

SOX focuses on internal controls over financial reporting (ICFR). Any workflow that touches invoices, approvals, reconciliation, or financial documentation needs to follow SOX-aligned processes: who approved what, when, and whether the record can be altered after the fact.

How docstoc supports SOX

In the product today (Business).

Maker-checker send control Optional SoD: a second teammate must approve before a chase is marked sent. Reminders never auto-send from docstoc.
Evidence-ready audit trails Actor-attributed chase events, SOX action log, and daily Bitcoin-anchored hash chains (including open/click tracking).
Segregation of duties Workspace Admin / Member roles plus maker ≠ checker on send approvals when SoD is enabled.
Period evidence packs Export a date-range HTML pack for auditors: invoices, chase timeline, SOX actions, and anchors.
Document certificates SHA-256 certificates with OpenTimestamps verification for immutable document evidence.
