Why ISO 27001 matters

ISO 27001 defines how organizations protect information, enforce access control, and maintain audit-ready processes. For finance teams handling invoices and documents, it means controlled access, secure storage, and continuous monitoring — not a one-time checklist, but controls that keep operating (and keep being evidenced) between annual surveillance audits.
