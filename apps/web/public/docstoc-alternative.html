FAQ

What happened to the original docstoc?

The original docstoc was a document-sharing site (with a companion video site, docstoctv) founded in 2007 that grew to over 20 million documents before Intuit acquired it for roughly $50M in December 2013 and shut it down on December 1, 2015. Today's docstoc.io is a new, independent product built by RELACON GmbH — not affiliated with or a continuation of the original company — reviving the free-template idea under the same name.

Is docstoc back in 2026?

Yes — docstoc is back with a free document template library covering similar ground (business, legal, real estate, finance, and HR templates), all free to copy with no signup, plus a free document verification/certification tool the original docstoc never had.

Are docstoc's templates reviewed by professionals?

Some are — templates submitted by a lawyer or accountant and approved with a verified-expert credential are marked as such. Others are general-purpose starting drafts. Always review any legal or financial document with a licensed professional before relying on it.
