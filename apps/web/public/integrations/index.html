Integrations

Invoice follow-up, wherever your invoice already lives

docstoc is copy-paste based, not deeply integrated into your workflow — which is exactly why it works from wherever you already keep invoices. Connect once, pull the file in, get a tone-matched follow-up.

Google Drive → Import invoice PDFs from Drive straight into the Tool. Gmail → Drafts save into your Gmail Drafts folder — you review and send. OneDrive → Import invoice PDFs from OneDrive / Microsoft 365. Box → Import invoice PDFs from Box straight into the Tool.

Also connects

Beyond cloud storage, docstoc syncs with QuickBooks and Xero to pull overdue invoices into your aging list, and supports CSV import and Zapier for everything else. Every draft is reviewed by you before it goes out — docstoc never auto-sends.

Which connection suits which situation

Choose by where the invoice lives today, not by what looks most advanced. If the PDFs sit in a folder, a cloud storage connection lets you pull one in without retyping. If you already send from Gmail, the Gmail connection puts the draft where you work. If an accounting system holds the truth, QuickBooks or Xero brings overdue invoices into your list.

What stays under your control

Connecting a service does not hand docstoc your outgoing mail. Every draft waits for your review, and you send it yourself.

Cloud storage import, accounting connectors and the Gmail draft feature belong to the paid plans. Pasting an invoice by hand and uploading a CSV work on the free tier.

  • Cloud storage: Google Drive, OneDrive, Box and Dropbox
  • Accounting: QuickBooks Online and Xero
  • Email: Gmail drafts
  • Everything else: CSV import and Zapier

If your tool is not on the list

A CSV export from a spreadsheet or invoicing tool is enough to get an aging list going. Webhooks, API keys and MCP access for draft generation are also available on the paid plans, for teams that want to build their own flow.

FAQ

Does any integration send email automatically?

No. docstoc never sends email on your behalf. Drafts are reviewed and sent by you.

Do I need a paid plan for integrations?

The cloud storage, accounting and Gmail features are on the paid plans. Manual entry and CSV upload are free.

Can I try it before connecting anything?

Yes. Paste one invoice by hand and read the draft first.
