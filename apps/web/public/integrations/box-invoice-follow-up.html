The problem

Client hasn't paid in 30 days. The invoice PDF is saved in Box, filed away weeks ago and mostly forgotten until now. Finding it, remembering the terms, and writing something that isn't awkward takes longer than it should.

The manual way

Dig through Box for the right file, reopen it to check the amount and due date, then write the email from scratch — trying to land somewhere between "friendly reminder" and "please pay me."

The docstoc way

Connect Box once in your docstoc workspace. From then on, pull an invoice PDF straight from Box into the Tool — no re-typing amounts or due dates — and get an AI-drafted follow-up matched to how overdue it is. You review it, then send it from your own inbox. docstoc never sends anything on your behalf.

What you need before importing from Box

The import reads an invoice PDF, so it works best when the file is a clean copy of what the client received. Check that each PDF in Box shows an invoice number, a due date and a total, because those are the details the follow-up draws on.

Putting the client name and invoice number in the file name saves you opening several files later.

From the PDF to the email you send

After the import you review the amount and due date, then read the draft. The tone follows how many days the invoice is overdue, so a note two days late reads differently from one at forty days.

Copy the text into your own email client and attach the PDF. Nothing is sent from docstoc.

  • Check the client's contact name and address
  • Confirm the invoice is still unpaid
  • Edit anything that does not sound like you
  • Add a payment date you are asking for

Plan and privacy notes

Cloud storage import is part of the paid plans. If you would rather not connect Box yet, you can paste the invoice details by hand or upload a CSV and get the same kind of draft.

FAQ

Does docstoc send the PDF from Box to my client?

No. The import only pulls the invoice into docstoc to build the draft. You send the email yourself.

Do I need a paid plan to import from Box?

Yes. Cloud storage import is a Pro feature. The free tier supports manual entry and CSV upload.

What if the invoice is not in a PDF?

Paste the key details by hand: client name, amount and due date.
